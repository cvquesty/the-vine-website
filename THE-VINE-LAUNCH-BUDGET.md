## GUIDING PHILOSOPHY

This is not a "build it and they will come" budget. This is a **portable, relational, low-overhead** launch budget designed for a bi-vocational planter in a small town of 14,000.

**Core convictions:**
1. **Leverage what we already have.** The website is done. Digital infrastructure, design, and much of the tech backbone can be handled in-house because of vocation and calling. Those line items are zeroed or minimized.
2. **Portable first.** No building purchase. No long-term lease. We rent space weekly or monthly (school gym, community center, theater) and we own the tools to set up and tear down fast and well.
3. **Frugal but not cheap.** We buy quality that lasts 7–10 years (good audio, solid trailer, real chairs). We buy used where it makes sense. We accept donations and discounts. We avoid debt.
4. **Relationships over production.** Every dollar either helps us gather (chairs, space, signage), helps us sound like we care (audio, lighting, hospitality), or helps us serve the community (outreach seed, launch team training).
5. **Setup/Tear-down reality.** The trailer and related gear exist because we are a mobile church that goes to people. That capability is non-negotiable.

The $100,000 target aligns with the existing Fundraising Plan ($80K personal + $20K district match). This document is the spending plan for those funds.

- Many "nice to have" items can wait for organic growth or specific donations.

## KEY ASSUMPTIONS & FRUGALITY NOTES

- **Digital moved / zeroed:** Website, most software development, graphic design, some streaming/setup, and tech consulting are provided at or near cost by the planter's vocation and existing infrastructure. This is the single biggest savings vs. a typical plant.
- **Bi-vocational planter:** No full salary in launch budget. Modest support appears in operating budget. This keeps the plant lean and the planter embedded in the community.
- **Used + network + DIY:** Trailer, chairs, some instruments, cases, and furniture will be sourced used where condition is excellent (Marketplace, church sales, liquidation, personal networks). Core team contributes skills (wiring, shelving, painting, transport).
- **Portable philosophy:** Every equipment decision favors fast setup (<45 min for full room) and fast teardown (<30 min) so we can use free or low-cost spaces and still feel excellent.
- **Kids start modest:** Focus on safety, cleanliness, and warm welcome. Production (sound/video) is secondary to having caring, background-checked adults in the room.
- **Signage is visibility:** In a small town, people drive by the same intersections every day. Good yard signs and a clean trailer with branding do more than one expensive billboard.
- **No debt:** If a line item goes over, we delay or find a cheaper alternative (rent chairs for launch, borrow a trailer for first month, etc.). The fundraising plan already avoids the $50k national loan option.

## WHAT THIS BUDGET DOES NOT INCLUDE

- Permanent building purchase or major renovation (future capital campaign)
- Full-time planter salary or benefits (phased into operating as attendance grows)
- Expensive video production or broadcast gear (start simple; phone + good audio first)
- Paid staff beyond planter (volunteers and bi-vocational leaders first)
- Large paid advertising after launch (organic + low-cost channels per PUBLICITY-GUIDE.md)

## STEWARDSHIP COVENANT

Every dollar given to this launch budget is a gift of trust. We will:
- Track every expense publicly (quarterly updates to donors)
- Buy once, maintain well, replace only when truly necessary
- Prioritize relationships and mission over shiny objects
- Return or reallocate any significant surplus to operating or mercy ministry

The goal is not a perfect launch. The goal is a **sustainable, debt-free, relationally rich** church that can still be excellent in borrowed spaces for years while we grow roots in Athens.
